I’m **Bhakti Pradhan**, a technology architect with around 20 years of experience across software engineering, technical leadership and solution architecture in banking and financial services.

My career has moved from Java development and engineering leadership into designing enterprise solutions, digital banking capabilities and complex integration landscapes. I enjoy connecting business outcomes with technology choices — and making architecture understandable, actionable and resilient.

## What I focus on

- **Solution and enterprise architecture:** translating business needs into target-state designs, architecture decisions and practical delivery roadmaps.
- **Digital banking:** customer journeys, onboarding, account opening, API-led integration and modernisation.
- **Enterprise engineering:** microservices, orchestration, distributed systems, security, resilience and governance.
- **AI architecture:** exploring RAG, agentic systems, MCP, model integration, evaluation and the controls needed to use AI responsibly in enterprise environments.

## Why Drop of Knowledge?

Technology changes quickly. Good architectural thinking remains grounded in fundamentals: understand the problem, make trade-offs explicit, protect users and data, and design for change.

This site is my public learning journal — a place to explain concepts, document hands-on experiments, draw architecture diagrams and share questions worth exploring. Some articles will come from established experience; others will document learning in progress. I will aim to be clear about the difference.

## A few principles I value

1. **Clarity over complexity.** Architecture should help teams make better decisions.
2. **Security and trust by design.** Controls should be part of the architecture, not an afterthought.
3. **Evidence over hype.** Evaluate technology against real requirements and measurable outcomes.
4. **Continuous learning.** Build, test, reflect and share.
